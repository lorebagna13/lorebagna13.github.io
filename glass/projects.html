TAOG

The AI of GOAT

A bot trained to play the GOAT format of Yu-Gi-Oh! (cards frozen at 2005), learning first by imitation and then by self play on top of a real rules engine. The goal is to bring something new to the community: use the bot to learn tactics, test decks, or see what it recommends in a given situation. Play a duel against it at theaiofgoat.com.
